Coverage Guide & Pro Tips

1 Coat vs 2 Coats

Use 1 coat for simple refresh projects of the same color. 2 coats ensure durability, accurate color richness, and full hiding power.

Surface Texture Impact

Unprimed, rough, or textured surfaces absorb significantly more liquid. Always factor in extra paint for brick, stucco, or heavy texture.

Standard Openings

Subtracted automatically: Doors (~21 sq ft / 2 sq m) and Windows (~15 sq ft / 1.4 sq m) so you don't overbuy paint.

The 10% Rule

Always keep a small leftover portion of your mixed batch for future edge touch-ups and repair patches down the road.
